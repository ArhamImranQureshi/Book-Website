A Story of Courage, Compassion & Faith

Sgt. Edward G. Lindberg served as a U.S. Army Medical Technician (MOS 409) in the European campaign. He treated the wounded, comforted the suffering, and shared the hope of Christ amid war.

This blog shares the book chapter by chapter, with the family photographs, documents and history behind it — a portrait of sacrifice, service and remembrance for readers of World War II history, military medicine and inspirational biography.
